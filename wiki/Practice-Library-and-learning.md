## Review Framework (AOS)

**Repo:** [aigovops-foundation/ai-govops-foundation-audit-v1](https://github.com/aigovops-foundation/ai-govops-foundation-audit-v1) · **Stack:** React/TS + Supabase · **Status:** Archived

A policy bundle runs through agent reviewers (linter, risk-scorer, framework-mapper, scenario analyst). A chartered human then issues an Attestation of Conformance, and every step goes into an HMAC-SHA256 audit chain. Defines the AiGovOps Operational Standard (AOS) v0.1: 18 controls across 7 domains. Built on Lovable.

**Place in the practice:** Early (April) precursor of the policy and audit gates.

- Links point to `aigovopsfoundation.org` and `bobrapp/openclaw-installer`.
